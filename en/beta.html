Free beta testers

We’re looking for free beta testers

MirrorSsok lets you view and control many Android phones from one Windows PC. It’s a free beta, and we’d like feedback from people who test apps, or run a store, social accounts, or a shop, on many phones.

Who it’s for

If this sounds like your work, please apply.

People who test apps on many phones

Anyone who repeats the same steps on phones with different models or Android versions.

People who run a store or social accounts on many phones

Anyone who uses many phones for store demos, shopping, or social accounts.

Small shops

Shops with several phones that would rather view and control them from one PC.

What testers get

It’s free to use right now

There’s no charge during the beta. Download the Windows installer and start right away.

Consistent feedback comes with a benefit

If you send feedback consistently, you’ll get the paid features free for a period after the official launch. We’ll tell you the length of that period separately.

Your feedback shapes the product

Tell us what’s awkward, what you’d like to see, or which macros you use often, and we’ll use that to improve the product.

The benefit is offered to people who send feedback consistently. Applying alone does not guarantee it.

How to apply

Fill in your name, email, number of phones, intended use and device models on the form. We review applications on weekdays, 10:00–17:00 KST, and usually reply by email within 1 business day.

You can get the installer on the download page. Send problems you run into on the Feedback page.
