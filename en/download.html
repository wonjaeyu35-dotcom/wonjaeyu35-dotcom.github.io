Install and connect

1

Download and install

Run the Windows installer. It installs for your account with no admin rights, and nothing is installed on your phones. If SmartScreen shows an “Unknown publisher” warning, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

2

Turn on USB debugging

Tap the build number 7 times to unlock Developer options, then turn on USB debugging. The in-app guide helps for each model.

3

Connect over USB or Wi‑Fi

Use a data-capable cable and tap “Allow USB debugging”. On Android 11 or later you can also pair wirelessly with a QR code.

4

Select and act together

Connected phones appear in the grid. Organize them with groups and tags, and with Pro send the same action to many at once.

Because the installer is not code-signed, Windows SmartScreen may show an “Unknown publisher” warning or a “protected your PC” message. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
