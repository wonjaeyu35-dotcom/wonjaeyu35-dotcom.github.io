widgetsFeatures

View, select, act together

MirrorSsok features grouped by what they do. Features marked Pro come with Pro. Organizing with groups and tags is free; Group control, which commands a whole group at once, is Pro.

View and control

grid_viewIn Free

Device grid

See every connected phone as a live tile, labeled by number, name, connection type (USB/Wi‑Fi) and battery.

open_in_fullIn Free

Device detail and mirror

Open one phone large and control it with mouse and keyboard. Includes quality presets and screen-off mirroring.

wifiIn Free

USB and wireless

Connect with USB debugging. Pair wirelessly with a QR code on Android 11+. No app needed on your phone.

folder_copyIn Free

File transfer and screenshots

Send files from your PC to many phones and capture screens instantly.

videocamPro

Screen recording

Record phone screens directly on your PC.

content_pasteIn Free

Clipboard sync

Paste text copied on your PC into phones. You can turn it off in Settings.

Many phones at once

select_allPro

Group control

Pick a group or several phones and send Home, Back or app launches to all at once. On Free you control one phone at a time.

syncPro

Sync mode

Mirror taps and swipes from one lead phone to the phones you choose.

keyboardPro

Text commands

Type the same text into many phones at once, in any language.

labelIn Free

Device groups and tags

Name phones and organize them into groups and tags. Sending one command to a whole group is Group control (Pro).

Automation

account_treePro

Workflows

Chain app launches, keys, text commands, waits and screenshots, then run them in one go.

touch_appPro

Macros (add-on)

Record and edit taps and swipes, including steps that find on-screen text and tap it.

replayPro

Per-phone results and rerun

See step-by-step results for each phone and rerun only the ones that failed.

storefrontPro

Store app demo & test pack

Install a test app, launch it and run a test macro on each phone. Only for testing and demoing your own apps or apps you are authorized to use.

Tools

appsPro

App manager

Install APKs on many phones and tidy up apps.

terminalPro

Advanced ADB

Send the same ADB command to many phones from a command panel.

troubleshootIn Free

Connection check

Finds cable, permission and driver issues step by step and explains them in plain words.
