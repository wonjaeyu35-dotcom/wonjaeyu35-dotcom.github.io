Step by step

From installing to working with many phones, follow along in order.

Contents
  1. Install
  2. Prepare your phone
  3. Connect
  4. Work with many phones
  5. Automate
  6. FAQ

1. Install

  1. Download the installer from the Download page and run it.
  2. Accept the license and check the install folder. No admin rights needed.
  3. Open MirrorSsok and sign in. Your 7-day Pro free trial starts, no card required.

2. Prepare your phone

  1. In Settings › About phone, tap Build number 7 times to unlock Developer options.
  2. Turn on USB debugging in Developer options. Menu names vary a little by manufacturer.
  3. No app needed on your phone.

3. Connect

  1. USB: Connect with a data-capable cable and tap “Allow USB debugging”.
  2. Wireless: On Android 11+, scan the QR code in Add device › Wireless.
  3. If it doesn’t connect, Connection check finds the cause step by step.

4. Work with many phones

  1. Name phones and organize them with groups and tags (Free). Select phones in the grid by number or by dragging.
  2. Use the toolbar to send Home, Back, app launches or text to all of them (Group control, Pro).
  3. Turn on Sync mode to repeat a lead phone’s actions (Pro).

5. Automate

  1. Save frequent steps as a workflow and rerun them with one click (Pro).
  2. Record taps and swipes as macros, added as an add-on (Pro).
  3. The store app demo & test pack is Only for testing and demoing your own apps or apps you are authorized to use.

Frequently asked questions

helpMy phone doesn’t show upexpand_more
Make sure the cable supports data and USB debugging is on. The app’s Connection check finds the cause step by step.
helpThe “Allow USB debugging” prompt doesn’t appearexpand_more
Unlock the phone and reconnect the cable. If it still doesn’t appear, revoke USB debugging authorizations in Developer options and reconnect.
helpHow many phones can I connect?expand_more
Free covers 2 phones; Pro has no limit. The practical number depends on your PC and USB ports or hubs.
helpDo I need to install an app on my phone?expand_more
No. No app needed on your phone. Just turn on USB debugging.
helpDoes it work with iPhone?expand_more
MirrorSsok supports Android phones. iPhone is not supported.
helpWireless keeps droppingexpand_more
With 3 or more wireless phones, quality is lowered automatically for wireless phones to reduce drops. Stay close to the router or use USB.
helpDoes screen data go over the internet?expand_more
No. Phone screens go only to your PC over USB or your local network. The internet is used just for sign-in and subscription checks.
