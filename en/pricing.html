Pricing FAQ

Do I have to pay during the beta?
No. There’s no charge during the beta.
Where do I send feedback or questions?
Use the form on the Contact page, or email wonjaeyu35@gmail.com.
What’s the difference between groups/tags and group control?
Naming phones and organizing them into groups and tags is free. Group control, which sends one command to a whole group, comes with Pro.
What if I change PCs?
Tell us through the contact form that you changed PCs. Pro can be used on up to 3 PCs.
