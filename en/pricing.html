Pricing FAQ

helpWhat happens when the trial ends?expand_more
You move to the Free plan with no automatic charge. You keep the 2 phones you used most recently, and you can change which 2 at any time. Other phones stay connected; only mirroring and control pause.
helpAre my workflows and macros deleted?expand_more
No. They’re kept as they are and only scheduled runs pause. Switch to Pro and pick up where you left off.
helpHow do I get a refund?expand_more
Within 7 days of your first payment, request it through the contact form (Billing and refunds). You’ll get a full refund.
helpHow much do I save with yearly billing?expand_more
$29.99 a year is 37% less than paying monthly for 12 months ($47.88), about $2.50 a month.
helpWhat’s the difference between groups/tags and group control?expand_more
Naming phones and organizing them into groups and tags is free. Group control, which sends one command to a whole group, comes with Pro.
helpWhat if I change PCs?expand_more
Tell us through the contact form that you changed PCs. Pro can be used on up to 3 PCs.
