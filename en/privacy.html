1. At a glance

MirrorSsok ("MirrorSsok") is made by an individual developer in Korea ("we", "us"). This policy explains in plain language what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your choices. We follow Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and aim to be reasonable under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

  • What is on your phones stays on your PC. We do not send it to the developer's servers. This covers screens, photos, messages and app data.
  • The program keeps your license, usage records and settings only on your PC. Today it sends nothing to our servers: no license check, update check, usage statistics or error reports.
  • The only information we receive is what you type into the website contact form.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.

2. What we collect and why

Area Data Purpose Retention
Website contact form Name, email, topic, message, whether you agreed to data collection Reply to you 1 year after we reply. 3 years for messages about payments, refunds, complaints or disputes (Korean e-commerce records)
Visiting the website and downloading the installer Access records such as IP address, time and browser details. GitHub, which serves the website and installer, records these under its own rules Serve the website and installer, security We do not receive or store these records. GitHub's policy applies
  • The contact form asks only for what we need. Please do not include sensitive data such as national ID numbers or card details.
  • The Free plan has no account. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • When a retention period ends, we delete the data unless the law requires us to keep it.

3. Other uses

We do not use your data for anything outside these purposes. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or profile you.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal data with third parties unless the law requires it or authorities request it through proper legal process.

5. Service providers and international transfers

We use the services of these companies to run the website. Their servers are outside Korea.

Recipient What they do Country Data When / how Retention
FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) Forwards what you write in the contact form to our email United States and other countries where the service runs Name, email, topic, message When you send a message, encrypted (HTTPS) As long as needed to forward it, under the service's own policy
GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages, GitHub Releases) Serves the website and the installer United States and other GitHub data centers Access records such as IP address and time When you open the website or download the installer, encrypted (HTTPS) Under GitHub's policy
  • If you do not want an overseas transfer, please do not use the website contact form. Access records created when you open the website or download the installer cannot be avoided. Core features of the program (mirroring, control) run on your PC after installation without any transfer.
  • Your message is delivered to our email and only we read it.
  • If we start charging for Pro, a payment provider such as Paddle may process your payment data as merchant of record. Before that starts, we will revise this policy to name the provider and list what it does and what data it receives.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our use of your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Use the contact page, choose "Privacy", and we will reply within 10 days. A legal guardian or authorized agent may also make a request.

  • Data in the program (license file, settings) exists only on your PC. It is removed when you uninstall and choose "Delete my data too".
  • You can also ask us to delete a message you sent through the contact form.

7. Deletion

We delete data without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose is met. Electronic files are erased so they cannot be recovered. Data we must keep by law is stored separately.

8. Security

  • The website and contact form communicate over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Only we can access the email account that receives contact messages.
  • MirrorSsok runs on your PC at 127.0.0.1 (your own computer) and creates a new token each time it starts, so other programs and other PCs cannot reach it.
  • Phone screens travel only to your PC, over USB or your local network.

9. Cookies

Our website uses only what the site needs to work. We use no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. You can still view the site if you block cookies. If we add analytics, we will revise this policy and notify you beforehand.

10. Children

MirrorSsok is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Contact and privacy officer

  • Operator: MirrorSsok (individual developer)
  • Privacy officer: the operator
  • Contact: use the website contact page and choose "Privacy".

If you need to report a violation or get advice, you can also contact:

  • KISA Privacy Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, dial 118 in Korea)
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972)
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office Cyber Crime Division (spo.go.kr, dial 1301 in Korea)
  • National Police Agency Cyber Bureau (ecrm.police.go.kr, dial 182 in Korea)

12. For users in the EU, EEA and UK (GDPR)

  • Legal basis: legitimate interests (replying to your contact message). We use the information only to reply to the person who wrote.
  • Rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawing consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing.
  • Transfers: your message may be transferred to Korea, the United States and other countries. We try to choose providers that use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Complaints: you may complain to your local data protection authority.

13. For California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

  • The data in section 2 is what we collect (identifiers, and commercial information in your message).
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.
  • You have the right to know, delete, correct, and not be treated differently for using these rights. Use the contact page in section 11.

14. What may change

If we introduce features through which we receive personal data, such as a server-side license check, update check, in-app feedback, usage statistics or error reports, or paid checkout, we will first revise this policy and notify you. We will also say what we collect and why, how long we keep it, and whether we need your consent.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on the website 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days before for changes that are unfavorable or significant.
