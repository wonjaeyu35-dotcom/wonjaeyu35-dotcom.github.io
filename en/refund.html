This policy applies if we start charging for Pro. Payments may be processed by a payment provider such as Paddle, acting as merchant of record, and refunds go back to your original payment method. If the payment method or provider changes, we will revise this policy and notify you before it starts.

1. At a glance

  • Full refund within 7 days of your first payment, no questions asked.
  • Cancel any time. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • We email you 7 days before renewal.
  • The 7-day trial needs no card. If you only use the trial, there is no charge and nothing to refund.

2. Pro subscription

2-1. Full refund within 7 days of the first payment

Ask within 7 days of the day you first pay for Pro and we refund the full amount. This applies to both monthly and yearly plans, even if you already used the program. This is at least as favorable as the 7-day withdrawal right under Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.

2-2. After 7 days

  • We do not refund a period that has already started on a pro rata basis. Instead, if you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • If something is wrong with the Service, see section 3.

2-3. Renewal payments

  • We email you 7 days before renewal, so please cancel before then if you do not want to renew.
  • The 7-day full refund in 2-1 does not apply to renewals. But if the mistake is ours (for example, you did not get the renewal notice, or you canceled and were still charged), we refund the renewal. Please contact us within 14 days of the charge.

2-4. Changing plans

  • If you switch from yearly to monthly, the monthly plan starts after your yearly period ends. The yearly fee you already paid covers that period.

3. Your legal rights

These apply regardless of the policy above.

  • If the Service is not as advertised or contracted, you may withdraw within 3 months of receiving it, or within 30 days of when you knew or could have known.
  • For consumer disputes you can seek help from the Korea Consumer Agency (kca.go.kr, 1372) or a similar body in your country.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Use the website contact page and choose "Billing and refunds". Tell us the email you paid with and your order number (it is on your receipt).
  2. We reply within 3 business days.
  3. Once approved, the payment provider returns the money to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may take about 3 to 10 business days to show it. We start the refund within 3 business days of your request.
  4. You can also request a refund from the payment provider directly, using the link in your receipt email.

5. After a refund

  • Your Pro license ends and you return to the Free plan. The program and your data stay.
  • Repeatedly getting refunds on trials or subscriptions in bad faith may lead to restrictions (Terms, section 7).
  • Currency and taxes are refunded on the basis the payment provider applied at payment. The amount you receive may differ slightly because of bank fees or exchange rates.
